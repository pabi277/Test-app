PaisaCalc is an offline EMI (loan installment) and GST calculator for India. It
was built so that the numbers people care about — their EMI, their repayment
schedule, their GST split — never require an account, a sign-in, or an internet
connection.

## Data the app stores on your device

Everything you type into PaisaCalc stays on your device:

- Loan details you save (amount, interest rate, tenure, prepayment settings) and
  your recent GST calculations are stored in the app's private database.
- Your theme choice and whether export is unlocked are stored in the app's
  private settings.
- Exported files (PDF or PNG) are written only where you send them: through
  Android's document picker, or to a private cache file that Android's share
  sheet passes to the app you choose.

We do not operate a server, and PaisaCalc has no account system. We cannot read,
collect, or delete this data for you because we never receive it. It is removed
when you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. Android cloud backup and
device-transfer backup are disabled for PaisaCalc's data, so it does not travel
to another device through those routes.

PaisaCalc does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone, phone, SMS,
calendar, or broad storage permissions, and it does not include analytics,
crash-reporting, or advertising-SDK tracking beyond what is described below.

## When PaisaCalc uses the network

The calculator itself never needs the network. EMI, prepayments, the
amortization schedule, and the GST tab all work in airplane mode.

Three optional features can use the network, and only when you use them:

1. **The optional rewarded ad.** Exporting a PDF or image is unlocked by one
   short rewarded ad, or by the one-time ad-free purchase. Google Mobile Ads
   (AdMob) serves that ad. The ads SDK is initialized only when you tap the
   unlock, and the app requests no ad before that.
2. **Privacy choices.** Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) checks your
   privacy choices on each launch and, where required, shows a consent form and
   a privacy-options entry point (in the app: the ⋮ menu → *Ads & ad-free* →
   *Privacy options*).
3. **The one-time ad-free purchase.** Google Play handles the payment; see
   "Purchases" below.

## Advertising

PaisaCalc shows **one ad format: a rewarded ad you choose to watch**. There are
no banners, no interstitials, no app-open ads, and no ad is ever shown between
you and a calculation. If no ad can be served — for example when you are
offline — export is unlocked anyway for the same 24-hour window.

When you choose to watch the rewarded ad, Google Mobile Ads may collect and
process data such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address (which can
indicate an approximate location), device and app information, and your
interaction with the ad, in order to serve, measure, and (where your choices
allow it) personalize advertising. That processing is carried out by Google
under its own privacy policy, not by us: we never receive your personal data or
ad identifiers.

- Google privacy policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>
- How Google uses data in partner apps: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites>
- Google's consent tooling (UMP): <https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy>

Where the law or Google's requirements call for consent, PaisaCalc asks for it
through the UMP consent form before any ad is requested. You can change your
choices at any time from the privacy-options entry point named above, when your
region requires one. You can also opt out of personalized advertising in your
Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

**Ad-free purchase.** If you buy the ad-free upgrade, PaisaCalc stops requesting
ads entirely, and no ad-related data is processed for you from that point on.

## Purchases

PaisaCalc offers **one one-time purchase** that removes ads. Google Play
processes the payment and manages the transaction; we never see your payment
details, card, or address. PaisaCalc stores only a single local flag on your
device recording that you own the upgrade, so export stays unlocked and no ads
are requested. You can restore an eligible purchase from the same dialog
(*Ads & ad-free* → *Restore purchase*); Play tells the app whether your account
owns it.

Google Play's own privacy policy applies to the transaction:
<https://policies.google.com/privacy>.

## Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer your data, because we do not receive it.
The only data leaving your device is the ad-related processing described above
(carried out by Google's ads SDK when you choose to watch an ad) and the Play
purchase flow. Exported files leave the app only to the destination you pick in
Android's share sheet or document picker.

## Retention and deletion

- **On your device:** kept until you delete the entry, clear the app's storage,
  or uninstall the app.
- **Ad-related data:** retained by Google according to the ads SDK's own
  retention rules; see the Google privacy policy linked above. We hold no copy.
- **Purchases:** Google Play keeps the transaction record; the app keeps only
  the local ownership flag, which is removed when you uninstall.

Because PaisaCalc has no account and no server, there is no account-deletion
process to request — uninstalling the app deletes everything the app stored.
For questions or requests about ad-related data, contact
[pabitra27.7.06@gmail.com](mailto:pabitra27.7.06@gmail.com) and we will point you
to the right Google process.

## Children

PaisaCalc is a financial calculator intended for adults managing loans and
taxes. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal
information from children. The app is not part of Google Play's Families
program.

## Changes to this policy

If PaisaCalc's data practices change, this page is updated and the effective
date above changes with it. Material changes are described in the app's release
notes on Google Play.
